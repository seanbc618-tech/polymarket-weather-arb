# Polymarket Weather 0.1.3

Welcome to Polymarket Weather 0.1.3! This release brings significant improvements to our weather market discovery, quantitative forecasts, and AI integration.

## New Features

* **3-Day Discovery:** The bot now discovers and analyzes D0, D1, and D2 weather markets, completely handling local-time settlement nuances across different timezones.
* **Expanded Data Sources:** Google Weather and multiple numerical weather models (GFS, ECMWF, ICON, GEM) now contribute to the quantitative probability estimates.
* **Calibrated LLM Vote:** We've introduced a measured AI advisor! The LLM now votes on weather markets, starting with a weight of zero. It only gains pricing influence after proving its accuracy across at least 20 resolved events.
* **Hardened Pricing Chain:** If the LLM produces a stale, invalid, or mismatched signal, it is safely ignored and will never block or affect live quantitative pricing.

## Dual Architecture Downloads

We now provide native applications for both **Apple Silicon** and **Intel** Macs. They are not universal binaries, so please download the correct one for your system.

* Apple Silicon: `Polymarket Weather-0.1.3-macos-arm64.dmg`
* Intel: `Polymarket Weather-0.1.3-macos-x86_64.dmg`

**Which one do I need?**
1. Click the **Apple Menu** in the top-left corner of your screen.
2. Select **About This Mac**.
3. If it says **Apple M1/M2/M3** (or similar), download the `arm64` version.
4. If it says **Intel**, download the `x86_64` version.

*Note: These builds are currently ad-hoc signed and not notarized. You may need to right-click and select **Open** on the first launch.*

## Upgrade Instructions

Upgrading is easy and completely safe for your data.

1. **Quit** the old app completely from its menu-bar menu.
2. Open the new DMG file.
3. Drag the new **Polymarket Weather.app** into the Applications folder and choose **Replace**.
4. Your config and database remain in `Application Support`; secrets remain in macOS Keychain.
